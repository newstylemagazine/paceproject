How your story is used

Pilot version, under review. This notice will be finalized before Trace accepts stories publicly.

What we collect

What you type into this form: your answers, the details about your studies, and, if you give them, your name and email. We don't collect anything else about you, and we don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why

To add your story to Trace's archive, where current graduate students read it, search it, and get replies that quote it. Your email is used only to contact you about your story.

Who sees it

  • Before approval: only the Trace reviewers.
  • After approval: anyone using Trace, under the name you chose. Your email is never shown.
  • Automatic screening: when you submit, your answers are sent to Trace's AI provider (Groq, in the United States) to flag things a reviewer should look at, such as contact details or other people's names. Stories are also sent to the AI provider when students' replies quote them.

Where it's stored

On Netlify, Trace's hosting provider, on servers that may be outside Canada.

If you dictate

The microphone button uses your browser's own speech recognition (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari, Microsoft in Edge), which may process your voice on that company's servers. Trace never receives a recording, only the text that appears in the box, which you can edit before sending.

Your choices

Your story stays yours: you give Trace permission to show and quote it, and you can ask for it to be corrected or removed at any time. To do that, or to ask what we hold about you, contact the Trace team at [contact address to be added before launch].

Please don't include

The names of people who wouldn't want to be named (a supervisor, a classmate, family), or anything you wouldn't want public. You're welcome to mention the people who shaped your experience; if you're unsure whether someone would mind, describe them by role instead ("my supervisor"). Reviewers may edit details like these, with your OK if you left an email.

If you're struggling right now, please reach out to someone you trust or a local support service; this form isn't monitored in real time.
